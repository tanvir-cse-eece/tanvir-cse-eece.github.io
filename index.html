Research Statement

I am a dedicated AI researcher pursuing my M.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering at BRAC University, with a foundational B.Sc. in Electrical, Electronic and Communication Engineering from MIST. My research centers on developing trustworthy and robust machine learning systems that can be deployed safely in real-world applications.

My work spans multiple interconnected domains: adversarial robustness and data poisoning detection in foundation models, graph-based reasoning systems for knowledge verification, and efficient multimodal AI for resource-constrained environments. I am particularly interested in the intersection of ML security and interpretability.
